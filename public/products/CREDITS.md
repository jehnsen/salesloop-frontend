# Product photo credits

Official DXN packshots from the DXN product catalogue (https://www.dxn2u.com/product/),
downloaded 2026-10-24. Each photo is trimmed to the product and centred on an 800×800
white canvas (WebP). The "DXN's Property" watermark is part of the original files.

Packaging note: the photos follow the price sheet's thumbnails.

- `lingzhi-3-in-1-classic*` uses DXN's yellow "3 in 1 Lite" pouch.
- `cordyceps-coffee*` uses DXN's "Cordyceps Coffee 3 in 1" pouch, although the price sheet lists the product as 4-in-1.

Replace these files if the distributor supplies their own photos.
